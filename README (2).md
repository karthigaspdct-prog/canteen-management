# ABC Masala & Pickles - Online Shop

A simple online shop website for homemade masala powders, pickles and malt. Customers pick products, add them to a cart and send the order on WhatsApp. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No installation or backend needed.

## Products
- ABC Malt
- Fish Kulambu Masala Powder, Fish Fry Masala, Chicken Masala, Mutton Masala, Turmeric Powder
- Mango, Lemon, Garlic and Tomato Pickles

## Features
- Category filter (Malt, Masala Powders, Pickles) and search (English and Tamil)
- Different weights and prices for each product
- Shopping cart with quantity change and remove
- Order summary sent through WhatsApp with name, phone and address
- Copy order button
- Cart saved in the browser (localStorage)
- Works on mobile and laptop

## How to Edit
Open `script.js`. The top of the file has the settings:
- `SHOP_NAME`: your shop name
- `SHOP_WHATSAPP`: your WhatsApp number with country code, for example `919876543210`
- `PRODUCTS`: change product names, prices and sizes (the prices are sample prices)
